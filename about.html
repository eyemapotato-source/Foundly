ABOUT FOUNDLY

Lost Things.
Found Connections.

FOUNDLY is a lost-and-found platform created to make reporting, searching, and recovering lost belongings easier for the school community.

🎯

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide one accessible place where people can report lost or found belongings and easily search for items that may belong to them.

🔎

How It Works

Users can report a lost or found item, provide its description, date and location, and search through reports to find possible matches.

🛡️

Safe Connections

FOUNDLY helps connect people who lost belongings with people who found them while encouraging users to only share contact information they are comfortable providing.
